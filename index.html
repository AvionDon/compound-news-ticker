Put it in Meld Studio

  1. Upload index.html to Netlify Drop (same as the death counter) — or open it from your PC.
  2. In Meld: add a Browser layer, paste the ticker URL below, set it to 1920 × 90 and park it at the bottom of your scene.
  3. Hover the ticker inside Meld and hit Link .txt file (live), pick your ticker.txt once. After that just edit and save that file — the scroll updates itself.
